## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a tactile, modern luxury gifting aesthetic tailored specifically for women celebrating cherished personal milestones and thoughtful everyday moments across India. The visual atmosphere evokes the unwrapping of an artisan gift box: layered silk tissues, hand-tied satin ribbons, and warm, delicate fragrance.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Warm Tactility & Modern Femininity:** Moves beyond sterile minimalism into deeply comforting, textural luxury. The interface feels inviting, soft, and celebratory without leaning juvenile or saccharine.
- **Thoughtful Emotional Tone:** Every interaction reinforces joy, affection, and anticipation—captured in the spirit of heartfelt gifting.
- **Cultural Resonance:** Styled to balance contemporary global boutique e-commerce with local celebration traditions (festivals, weddings, self-care rituals, sisterhood), using refined Indian Rupee (₹) typographic treatments and opulent champagne accents.
- **Physical Motifs:** UI accents integrate organic botanical line flourishes, fine scalloped container separators, delicate sparkle/twinkle indicators, and gentle ribbon pill containers for tags and badges.

## Colors

The palette balances creamy warmth with romantic rose, ethereal lavender, and luminous celebratory metallics. 

### Palette Application
- **Primary Rose Petal (`#D9657B`):** Acts as the foundational action color for primary CTA buttons, selected states, progress meters, and key highlighted badges. Softened tints (`#E88D9C`, `#F4A7B9`) are reserved for interactive hover states, subtle gradient fills, and decorative backdrops.
- **Secondary Lavender Mist (`#9B6BB0`):** Used for curatorial callouts, secondary actions, bundle indicators, and customizer accents. Tints (`#D8B4E2`, `#EADCF8`) serve as mood-tag backdrops and subtle category badges.
- **Tertiary Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Conveys premium craftsmanship, VIP curation tiers, festive luxury markers, and star ratings. Subtle gold foil tint (`#F5E8C7`) powers high-value promotional strips and gilded gift tags.
- **Accents & Freshness:** Sage Mint (`#D4E7DC`) provides an organic counterweight for in-stock statuses, organic/vegan product badges, and free shipping counters.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Background Canvas: Layered warm cream (`#FAF6EE`) and whipped buttercream (`#FDFBF7`, `#FFFDF9`).
  - Elevated Cards: Pure pearlescent white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with whisper-soft blush borders (`#F3E8E8`).
  - Text & Hierarchy: Deep Plum Espresso (`#2D1F29`) replaces stark harsh black, ensuring lush legibility with warmth. Muted mauve (`#7A6672`) serves secondary metadata and caption roles.

## Typography

Typography bridges timeless high-fashion editorial presence with effortless digital readability.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs infuse the browsing experience with the grace of high-end editorial magazines. Italicized styling is used deliberately on key emotional phrasing (e.g., *Curated with Love*, *The Birthday Edit*).
- **Body, Inputs & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its generous apertures and soft geometric shapes echo the roundness of the visual theme while maintaining strict functional legibility at high data density.
- **Price & Currency Styling:** The Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) always matches the font weight and tracking of its accompanying numeral value to prevent visual detachment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop views, shifting to a 6-column grid for tablets and a 4-column layout for mobile viewports.

- **Viewport Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (6 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max content container `1280px`, `margin: 3.5rem`, `gutter: 2rem`)
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Breathing room is prioritized around visual merchandise and photography. Generous padding (`space-xl` and above) surrounds hamper display tiles to emulate boutique showroom showcases.
- **Safe Areas & Fixed Mobile Bars:** An explicit bottom safe-padding offset of 80px is integrated across mobile views to prevent interference from floating sticky add-to-cart drawers and navigation hubs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth departs from cold, desaturated drop shadows in favor of warm, petal-tinted ambient glows and layered porcelain cards.

- **Base Ambient Shadow:** Surfaces sit on a warm, dual-layer shadow using `#483441` (Plum) tinted down to 4% opacity blended with a rose bloom tone (`#D9657B`) at 6% opacity:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(72, 52, 65, 0.04), 0 8px 24px 0 rgba(217, 101, 123, 0.06);`
- **Elevated Interactive Hover:** Elevates cards and interactive tiles by expanding the rose blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(72, 52, 65, 0.06), 0 16px 36px 0 rgba(217, 101, 123, 0.12);`
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Bars:** Drawers, quick-view modals, and mobile cart navigation docks utilize a soft backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) across a 90% opaque pearlescent buttercream fill, held by a whisper-fine outline (`border: 1px solid #F3E8E8`).
- **Surface Layering:** Rather than heavy elevation steps, depth is primarily communicated horizontally through tinted containers: Base (`#FAF6EE`) -> Card (`#FFFFFF`) -> Inner Inset / Gift Item Well (`#FDFBF7`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-soft, organic contour language. The pill-shaped baseline creates a welcoming, friendly, and tactile touch environment.

- **Primary Components:** Action buttons, chips, search pills, quantity selectors, and product tags leverage full pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Product & Content Containers:** Hamper cards, modal dialogs, and image carousels use smooth large corner radii (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `24px` to `32px`), visually mimicking custom confectionery boxes and cosmetic packages.
- **Accents & Icons:** Line art, decorative dividers, and badge framing incorporate soft arc terminal points and circular gemstone pins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gift/Checkout):** Full pill-shape button filled with Deep Rose (`#D9657B`), white text, and a subtle inner highlight. Includes smooth spring hover with elevated pink ambient shadow.
- **Secondary (Customizer/Secondary Action):** Pearlescent white background, framed with a 1.5px border in `#E88D9C`, text set in `#2D1F29`.
- **Festive/VIP Tertiary:** Champagne Gold gradient fill (`#F5E8C7` to `#D4AF37`) with deep plum typography for limited festival bundles.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill containers with heights of 28px–32px.
- **Tags:** Sage Mint (`#D4E7DC`) for sustainability or freshness tags; Soft Lavender (`#EADCF8`) for gifting occasion labels (e.g., *Bridesmaid*, *Rakhi*, *Self-Love*).
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Unselected states feature whipped cream background with `#F3E8E8` border; selected states feature solid Plum Espresso (`#2D1F29`) or Rose Petal (`#D9657B`) with white text.

### Product & Hamper Cards
- Pearlescent white base surface, bordered with delicate `1px solid #F3E8E8`, rounded to 24px (`rounded-3xl`).
- Imagery features soft 4:5 aspect ratio with subtle zoom on hover.
- Includes floating circular wish-list icon button at top right with gentle rose backdrop tint.
- Pricing row places bold Indian Rupee (`₹`) text prominently alongside an optional crossed-out comparison MRP and an understated badge for savings.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill or rounded-2xl text fields with 48px height for effortless thumb access on mobile.
- Buttercream fill (`#FFFDF9`) surrounded by a subtle boundary line (`#F3E8E8`). On focus, smoothly transitions to a 1.5px Rose Petal border with a 4px soft bloom ring (`rgba(217, 101, 123, 0.15)`).
- Placed placeholder labels in muted mauve (`#7A6672`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Radio buttons feature smooth circular concentric rings; when active, they display a solid Rose Petal center surrounded by whipped white and a rose outer track.
- Checkboxes maintain a generous 6px corner radius with an animated checkmark flourish in soft gold or white.

### Specialized Gifting Components
- **Gift Note & Wax-Seal Toggle:** Custom switch modeled after a textured wax seal that unfurls a handwritten typography input box.
- **Floating Mobile Gifting Bar:** A persistent bottom bar pinned on mobile screens featuring an active hamper summary counter, current value (₹), and a vibrant "Send as a Gift" checkout button.
- **Hamper Step Builder ("Build-a-Box"):** Segmented progress tabs housed within a pill enclosure, showing miniature thumbnail badges as items are added.
